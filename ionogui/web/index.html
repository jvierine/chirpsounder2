Quick help

Sounders: Digisonde, LFM (chirp) and PRN (coded). Availability varies by receiver.

Choose a UTC day and time; green calendar days have archived data. Now follows today’s live data. The map uses the last observation at or before the selected time; RTIs and spectra show the full day.

Drag station tabs to rearrange them. To reorder plots, open ⚙ and use the compact Arrange plots list; both orders are saved in this browser.

Drag inside a plot to pan; pinch with two fingers or Ctrl/⌘ + scroll to zoom. On a phone, ☰ opens the map’s date, receiver and settings controls. Double-click to reset. Click a colorbar to adjust its limits.

Choose a receiver tab or the all-station Propagation tab. Each receiver tab reports CPU use and remaining headroom. ⚙ selects products and layout.

Click a radio link for its last 48 hours: time versus range coloured by frequency, and time versus frequency coloured by range. Drag to pan, Ctrl/Cmd + wheel to zoom; click either colorbar to adjust its limits. Latest ionogram opens the individual sounding.

PRN ionograms show sparse echoes above 8 dB that also pass the transmitter-code check. Blank cells are rejected or unmeasured. Automatic band estimates still require 13 dB.

Cyprus, Australia and the ROTHR transmitter sites at New Kent (Virginia), Premont (Texas) and Vieques (Puerto Rico) use sweep detections in expected range gates. Site associations and operational usable frequencies remain unverified. Overlapping ROTHR gates are marked as unresolved candidates and do not supply quantitative frequency limits. W2NAF Australia and Cyprus measurements are excluded from quantitative use because of cross-modulation; their diagnostic plots require an explicit opt-in. W2NAF–ROTHR measurements retain a limited-reliability flag.

RTI views share a time axis. Regular soundings fill their normal display interval; longer outages stay white. Shaded sweep bands show expected transmitter ranges.
