Quick help

Choose a UTC day. Drag station tabs to rearrange them. To reorder plots, open ⚙ and use the compact Arrange plots list; both orders are saved in this browser.

Drag inside a plot to pan; Ctrl/⌘ + scroll to zoom. Double-click to reset. Click a colorbar to adjust its limits.

Choose a receiver tab or the all-station Propagation tab. Each receiver tab reports CPU use and remaining headroom. ⚙ selects products and layout.

RTI views share a time axis. Regular soundings fill their normal display interval; longer outages stay white. Shaded sweep bands show expected transmitter ranges.
